Physiology notes: Values are illustrative, not fixed clinical measurements. The systemic panel deliberately uses the classical Starling model (CHP − COP); modern revised Starling physiology generally predicts little sustained venous reabsorption and emphasizes the endothelial glycocalyx and lymphatic return. The glomerular panel uses NFP = PGC − PBS − πGC, with Bowman's space oncotic pressure assumed ≈ 0. To match the reference figure, the blue glomerular curve shows effective hydrostatic pressure (PGC − PBS) of 45→40 mmHg, corresponding to an actual glomerular capillary pressure of 60→55 mmHg when PBS = 15 mmHg. Glomerular filtration depends on filtration coefficient (Kf) as well as NFP. Hypoproteinaemia is represented by a lower starting oncotic pressure; in reality, its effects depend on additional haemodynamic factors.
